In Odoo 18, the *Download > PDF* menu and the portal of a posted
customer invoice that has not been sent yet return a document titled
PROFORMA, because the legal PDF is only generated and stored through the
*Send* wizard.

This module removes the PROFORMA title and filename suffix from that
document, so it is printed as a regular invoice. The document is still
rendered on the fly, so it always reflects the current state of the
invoice (e.g. registered payments), and no attachment is stored.

The proforma stored by the *Send* wizard as a fallback when the
electronic document generation fails is kept as is.

Usage
=====

1. Go to *Invoicing > Customers > Invoices* and open a posted invoice
   that has not been sent yet.
2. Click on *Download > PDF*.
3. The invoice PDF is downloaded without the PROFORMA title.
